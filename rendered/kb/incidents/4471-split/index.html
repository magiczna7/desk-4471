What each one is

  • parser (ticket, fixed): the shared parser read every date month first; every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month swapped, through app, email and booking-v2 forms. Fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint (ticket, won't fix): booking-v3 printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People typed what it said, and the form read it the tenant's way. Same symptom, different code: the parser fix did not touch it. The form is retired, so no new reports should come this way.
  • one-day move (ticket, open): no swap. The date moves by one day and the weekday with it, and one meeting showed on different days in two cities. That is time zones, not order, so neither change above fixes it. The desk files these reports under parser and footnotes them (desk-notes.json).

How they ended up in one ticket

Triage merges on subject line (decision 0001). All three problems produce the same subjects ("Wrong date again", "Month and day swapped?"), so the rule merged them without reading a body. Intake's tag does not help either: it guesses from the reporter's browser, not the tenant, and splits the hint reports between both tags.

For the desk

  • Split by cause before marking a big ticket known. A date ticket needs the tenant's locale, its booking form and what the two dates in the body do to each other.
  • When a big ticket closes, check what each of its causes got. Here one got a fix, one a retirement and one nothing.
