What each one is

  • parser (ticket): the shared parser reads every date month first; every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month swap, through app, email and booking-v2 forms. This is what #4471 describes.
  • hint (ticket): booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads it the tenant's way. Same symptom, different code: the parser fix does not touch it.
  • one-day-move (ticket): no swap. The date moves by one day and the weekday with it, and one meeting showed on different days in two cities. That is time zones, not order. The desk already noted these reports (desk-notes.json); #4471 still counts them under parser.

How they ended up in one ticket

Triage merges on subject line (decision 0001). All three problems produce the same subjects ("Wrong date again", "Month and day swapped?"), so the rule merged them without reading a body. Intake's tag does not help either: it guesses from the reporter's browser, not the tenant, and splits the hint reports between both tags.

For the desk

  • Split by cause before marking a big ticket known. A date ticket needs the tenant's locale, its booking form and what the two dates in the body do to each other.
  • Keep #4471 for parser. Move the other two to their tickets so they are not closed with it.
